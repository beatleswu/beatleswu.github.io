# E10-NV-1B — Newbie Village Art Direction Bible

## 1. Purpose

This is the production contract for the first complete Go Odyssey visual vertical slice: **Newbie Village**.
It turns the E10-NV-1A Owner decision into constraints that an artist, image-generation workflow,
reviewer, and later runtime integrator can independently verify.

Scene 01 (Awakening) and Scene 04 (Elder's Test) are retained reference material, not proof that
every aspect of either image is canonical. Scene 02 and Scene 03 must be replaced. Scene 05 is a
new persistent World Stage master scene. No image, manifest, Intro, audio, or runtime behavior is
changed by this document.

## 2. Product constitution

1. **Newbie Village first.** This is E10's first full vertical slice; do not borrow scope from later
   zones, Boss spectacle, or whole-site art.
2. **One Intro system only.** Retain the existing Intro, `adventure_intro_seen_v1`, MP3 narration,
   skip/finish/fallback semantics, and lifecycle. New art is a future input to that system, never a
   second cinematic, timeline, or audio manager.
3. **Go RPG, not generic fantasy.** Every production image must read as a young Go player's journey
   through an explorable RPG village. It must not collapse into a generic Western fantasy, wuxia,
   Japanese isekai, children's cartoon, chess site, or ungrounded AI splash art.
4. **Persistent world over disconnected stills.** The village must feel inhabitable: a player can
   linger, receive a quest, train, and leave toward the next region.

## 3. Canonical visual identity

**Positioning sentence:** *A dawn-lit starter village built around an ancient Way of Go: warm timber,
weathered stone boards, black-and-white currents, and modest adventurer equipment form a credible,
explorable RPG beginning.*

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Era | Timeless pre-industrial fantasy; hand-crafted tools, no modern objects or technology. |
| Geography | A terraced valley village at the forest edge, with a visible road leading toward the future Slime Plains. |
| Village function | Home, novice dojo, small quest square, supply/training point, elder's test court, and safe departure point. |
| Player identity | A novice Go apprentice and village traveller: capable enough to carry a wooden sword, not yet an elite hero. |
| Go in the world | A practiced art, a civic tradition, and a measured form of magic; it is embedded in places of meaning rather than pasted everywhere. |
| Magic | Black/white influence is a visible response to balance, intent, and position; it is restrained environmental energy, not neon spell spam. |
| Future connection | The north/east departure road, low grassland horizon, and distant forest provide a readable path toward Slime Plains and later zones. |

## 4. Main character canon

### 4.1 Age and silhouette

- Visual age: **15–17**; adolescent, not childlike and not an adult warrior.
- Build: slim, active novice; narrow shoulders, practical stance, no exaggerated muscles.
- Proportions: stylized-3D approximately 6.5–7 heads tall; natural limbs, expressive but non-chibi hands.
- Readable silhouette: tousled dark hair, short teal-blue outer tunic, cross-body strap, modest belt,
  wooden sword at left hip/back, and round brass-and-wood pendant.

### 4.2 Face and hair

- Face: soft oval with a gently tapered jaw; warm light-medium East Asian skin tone; no facial hair.
- Eyes: clear blue-grey, almond-shaped, alert rather than oversized anime eyes.
- Brows: dark, moderately thick, slightly arched; a key expression anchor.
- Hair: dark chestnut-black; layered tousle with forward fringe split slightly off centre; sides cover
  upper ears; short textured back at nape. Do not change to light brown spikes, a ponytail, or a fringe-free crop.

### 4.3 Outfit and materials

- Base: desaturated **teal-blue linen tunic** over an off-white collar/undershirt.
- Outer layer: short weathered blue-green shoulder mantle or simple travel vest; no cape.
- Belt/strap: dark brown leather cross-body satchel strap and waist belt with brass buckle.
- Lower body: clay-beige trousers, brown ankle boots, dark brown leather bracers.
- Identifier: round brass-and-wood pendant at sternum; it is the visual bridge between ordinary village life and Go resonance.
- Wear: clean enough for a starter village, with light scuffs at cuffs/boots only. No heavy armor, royal tailoring, school uniform, kimono, or high-mage robes.

### 4.4 Wooden sword

- Length: short training sword, roughly forearm-to-shoulder length when held; blunt single-piece warm ash wood.
- Guard: simple small oval/round dark-wood guard; no metal crossguard, gems, or ornate fantasy blade.
- Grip: dark brown cloth/leather wrap; small brass end cap.
- Mark: one subtle incised **star-point / Go-grid** motif near the guard, not a glowing logo.
- Carry: left hip or diagonal back sheath loop when not in use. It never changes length, wood color, guard shape, or handedness between scenes.
- Glow: no constant glow. A faint black/white reflected rim is permitted only when Go resonance is active.

## 5. Companion canon

- Chinese canonical art-direction name: **水靈馬**.
- English working name: **Water Spirit Horse**. This is art-direction working terminology, not an assertion that player-facing English i18n copy is complete.
- Role: Newbie Village's first canonical spirit companion: a quiet early-warning companion and emotional anchor, not a combat mascot, mount, or prize animal.
- Species/read: a small, juvenile water-element horse spirit. It is friendly and grounded enough for a starter village; it must not read as a large riding horse, elite divine beast, Boss, Western unicorn, seahorse, or final-game summon.
- Proportion: about forearm-to-waist height beside the adolescent hero; rounded juvenile proportions, short legs, a compact head, and large clear blue-grey eyes. It is never taller than the hero's shoulder or broad enough to imply riding.
- Body and colour: pale water-blue body with a restrained aqua-to-teal translucent water mane and tail; soft, clean water contours, a few small ripples/droplets, and no opaque armour or heavy particle effects.
- Go identity: one small black-and-white stone-shaped forehead marking or pendant, plus a subtle star-point accent. These are limited identifiers, never a full checkerboard coat.
- Forbidden traits: wings, horn, armour, weapon, fire, lightning-first theme, dark-undead styling, royal regalia, oversized anatomy, realistic warhorse anatomy, or a saddle/riding pose.
- Scene continuity: Scene 02 may show Water Spirit Horse as a quiet fate-resonance or awakening clue; it must not displace the hero/Elder beat. Scene 03 shows the same individual as the hero's witness during wooden-sword practice. Scene 05 may omit it so the persistent World Stage remains environment-first.
- Scene 01 handling: the retained Scene 01 is historical visual reference for the hero, morning tone, and intimate framing only. Its existing green creature must not be used as a canonical first-companion reference after this update. This documentation change does not authorize modifying Scene 01; any future role for Mori is outside this document and undecided.

## 6. Elder canon

- Role: **Village Elder and keeper of the first Go trial**, a living teacher, not an undefined ancient projection.
- Age/build: late 60s to early 70s; lean upright frame, calm gravity rather than frailty.
- Face: narrow face, warm tan skin, pronounced but kind brow, deep-set dark eyes, long white moustache and forked medium-long beard.
- Hair: white-grey hair gathered in a low half-knot; loose temple strands. Do not use Scene 02's unrelated high topknot silhouette.
- Outfit: layered deep indigo/blue-charcoal travel-dojo robe with muted warm-brown sash, weathered stone-grey inner layer, and one small brass/wood Go pendant or tally.
- Accessory: straight dark wood staff with a restrained circular stone/Go-grid cap; no wizard staff crystal.
- Contrast: the elder is dark, vertical, and still; the hero is teal, lighter, and in motion. Both share brass/wood detail and the same world materials.
- Scene 02 rule: if an elder-like guide appears, it is this same Elder. A different guide is allowed only if explicitly labelled a non-human ancestral Go spirit with a clearly distinct, non-human silhouette; never an ambiguous look-alike.

## 7. Newbie Village architecture canon

The village is **60% East-Asian Go-dojo vernacular, 25% original valley-adventurer practicality, 15% restrained universal fantasy utility**.

- Materials: warm cedar/brown timber frames, pale stone foundations, clay-plaster wall fields, dark slate/wood-shingle roofs, aged brass fixtures.
- Roofs: low-to-medium pitched tiled/slatted roofs with gently lifted eaves only; no shrine replicas, pagoda overload, castle turrets, or Tudor half-timber facades.
- Streets: irregular warm-grey stone path, low stone steps, moss edges, drainage rills, timber rail fences.
- Windows/signage: paper-and-wood lattice windows; simple symbol plaques or readable local language only in future final art. No Japanese/English placeholder text, brands, or unreadable glyphs.
- Landscape: vegetable plots, young maples/pines, grasses, a narrow stream or water channel, forested hills and a distant open-plains road.
- Scale: a walkable village square with two- to three-storey structures maximum; no metropolis and no tiny toy-town proportions.

## 8. Go visual language

Use these repeatable motifs sparingly and logically:

1. **Stone board trial dais** — a real, proportionally coherent Go grid in the Elder's court; only where play/training occurs.
2. **Black/white balance currents** — low-opacity opposing air currents or cloud bands during imbalance/resonance, not permanent weather.
3. **Star-point crest** — a four- or nine-star-point abstract emblem on dojo lintels, pendants, and civic markers.
4. **Incised grid lines** — shallow board-line geometry in a threshold, practice ground, or paving medallion; never across every wall/floor.
5. **Stone-shaped lantern/waymarker** — rounded black/white paired markers at meaningful routes, with no floating stones unless magic is active.
6. **Go-boundary seal** — short-lived black/white linework around a trial or training target when a move resolves.

Rules: stones lie on valid intersections unless deliberately animated by visible resonance; board grids are square, evenly spaced, and free of chess symbols. Black/white is a balance language, not candy decoration or constant checkerboard texture.

## 9. Color and lighting bible

| Palette | Direction |
|---|---|
| Village base | cedar warm brown, weathered stone grey, morning gold, leaf green, valley sky blue. |
| Hero | teal-blue 45%, off-white 20%, clay beige 15%, brown leather 15%, brass 5%. |
| Elder | indigo/blue-charcoal 55%, stone-grey 20%, warm brown 15%, brass/wood 10%. |
| Black Go energy | charcoal-black with soft cool blue edge, never purple neon. |
| White Go energy | warm ivory-white with a subtle gold edge, never clinical pure-white bloom. |
| Resonance | muted brass-gold, used as a bridge between black and white; no saturated magenta/cyan neon. |

Scene progression: 01 warm sunrise and dust; 02 cool-to-warm dramatic cloud break; 03 clear early afternoon training light; 04 low golden-hour solemnity focused on the board; 05 open late-morning exploratory light. Materials, hero palette, and village palette must remain stable throughout.

## 10. Rendering style lock

The canonical target is **high-quality stylized 3D animated adventure illustration**. Scene 04 is the closer target for world-scale lighting and Go trial material treatment; Scene 01 is the closer target for the hero's intimate emotional readability. Future art must combine those strengths.

Characteristics: cinematic but readable lighting, simplified believable materials, expressive faces, natural hands, adolescent/general-audience appeal, and layer-friendly environmental depth. Do not mix painterly concept art, flat 2D anime, photorealism, chibi, watercolor, Western dark fantasy, or generic mobile-game splash art.

## 11. Scene contracts

### Scene 01 — Awakening (KEEP)

- Preserve: warm dawn bedroom, hero face/hair/teal tunic/pendant, and intimate wake-up framing.
- Tolerate: crop is a cinematic close-up; it need not function as persistent scene art.
- Lock: hero's blue-grey eyes, dark tousled hair, teal fabric, and pendant. Its existing green creature is not the Water Spirit Horse canon and must not be copied into new production art.

### Scene 02 — Fate Whisper (REPLACE)

- Include the canonical hero in the same outfit; both hands or pendant reacting to restrained black/white currents; a visible village/road horizon; and the canonical Elder.
- Water Spirit Horse appears as a small, friendly awakening/resonance cue near the hero or forming quietly in the currents. It must use the canonical water-blue juvenile design and must not take over the narrative centre.
- Beat: an ancient call and imbalance, not a mural, costume transformation, or high-level magic.
- Compose for a 16:9 master with hero within the central 40% width and horizon/road remaining legible when cropped vertically.
- Reject: painterly protagonist, unrelated elder, elite hero gear, a riding/summoning spectacle, purple portal, readable or garbled text, or an all-symbolic prophecy image.

### Scene 03 — Wooden Sword Trial (REPLACE)

- Include the canonical hero, exact wooden sword, village training yard, and an effort/courage moment: recovering from a stumble, setting a stance, or striking a training target.
- Include one grounded Go-training cue: star-point foot markers, a board-line target, or a restrained balance seal.
- Include the same Water Spirit Horse as a small supportive witness; it retains the exact water-blue body, aqua-to-teal water mane/tail, blue-grey eyes, and Go forehead mark/pendant from Scene 02. It is not a mount or another animal.
- Reject: formal slime battle, monster swarm, Western castle, any readable/garbled text, heavy armor, explosion magic, victory-pose power fantasy, or riding imagery.

### Scene 04 — Elder's Test (KEEP)

- Preserve: canonical Elder/hero opposition, ancient stone Go-board trial surface, black/white stones, golden-hour gravity.
- Improve only later if necessary: framing can improve mobile legibility, but the trial narrative cannot be replaced by combat spectacle.
- Board rule: grid and stones must remain visually logical; no random extra fingers, crossed weapons, or impossible stone placement.

### Scene 05 — Persistent World Stage master (NEW)

- Character-light wide environment, **not an Intro still**.
- Include: main square/route, dojo, Elder's court area, small shop or quest point, forest/Slime Plains departure, Go-waystone or central board landmark, one future monster-entrance edge, and one NPC zone.
- Build visible foreground, midground, and background. Keep wide central environmental weight and reserve HUD/title/CTA safe zones.
- Reject: face close-up, packed character group, all-critical content on side edges, a cinematic action beat, or any composition that reads as a film frame.

## 12. Responsive composition contract

All production images must be reviewed at 16:9 desktop, 4:3 tablet, and approximately 9:16 mobile cover crop.

- **Hero face safe area:** central 30–70% horizontal / 18–68% vertical for character scenes.
- **Elder safe area:** Scene 02/04 must preserve a readable Elder face within central 20–80% horizontal after mobile crop, or use a deliberate alternate mobile composition.
- **Board safe area:** Scene 04's key intersections and both players' hands must remain in central 15–85% horizontal / 38–88% vertical.
- **Scene 05 title/HUD:** reserve upper centre and one upper side quadrant free of critical landmarks; reserve lower centre/right for CTA without covering the dojo, road, or waystone.
- **Scene 05 environmental anchor:** central 45% holds the square/waystone/route; important buildings cannot exist only at left/right extremes.
- Each final asset records desktop/tablet/mobile `object-position`; crop review is mandatory, not a post-hoc CSS rescue.

## 13. Rejection rules

Reject an asset immediately if any major condition occurs: incorrect hero face/hair/outfit/sword;
Water Spirit Horse mutation (wrong species, scale, colour, mane/tail, Go mark, or riding implication); incorrect Elder; non-canonical rendering style; unreadable or foreign/garbled text;
watermark/brand; chess icon; malformed board grid/stones; extra fingers; weapon intersections; broken
anatomy; sudden wholly Western/Japanese village; chibi/photorealism; unsafe responsive crop; Scene 03
as monster combat; Scene 05 as character close-up; or black/white Go imagery used as cheap decoration.
