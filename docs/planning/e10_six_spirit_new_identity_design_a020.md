## Authoritative current set

### Spirit #4 — Starpath Antlerling

- **Role:** `EXPLORATION`
- **Identity:** celestial deer / star-path exploration Spirit.
- **Silhouette:** deer head and body, luminous branching antlers, constellation
  markings, and a flowing star-path ribbon. It must remain friendly and
  youthful rather than becoming a divine stag, war deer, or Titan creature.

Evolution is newborn celestial fawn → young growing Antlerling → mature
Starpath Antlerling:

- **Stage I — newborn / newly awakened:** Very small resting body, short sparse
  antlers, early constellation lights, large curious eyes, minimal star-path
  energy, clearly immature proportions.
- **Stage II — juvenile / growing:** Taller but still young; antlers begin to
  branch, constellation pattern becomes clearer, star trail strengthens, and
  posture becomes more confident while remaining visibly younger than Stage
  III.
- **Stage III — mature final identity:** Preserve the Owner reference's elegant
  blue celestial deer, constellation markings, luminous branching antlers,
  flowing ribbon, and friendly Go Odyssey expression. No added armor, size
  inflation, ancient-god treatment, or extra evolution.

**Follower language:** light footstep/ribbon movement around the Hero's path,
a tiny constellation blink at a discovered route, and a short star-ribbon cue
at zone transition. It never obscures a node and is never a mount.

**Hero portrait:** Three-quarter face-and-antler crop with constellation marks
and a small ribbon arc; status framing may distinguish Owned/Locked/Active,
but the portrait remains the Owner-selected form on desktop, iPad, and mobile.

**Battle cue direction:** a short star-path shimmer only after server
settlement; it does not predict or display a correct answer or numeric power.

### Spirit #5 — 阿肥 / Fatty

- **Role:** `PRECISION`
- **Identity:** the Owner's real dog elevated into a Go Odyssey Spirit, not a
  generic fantasy canine.
- **Silhouette:** compact black/charcoal scruffy dog, recognizable muzzle,
  pale/white chest and paw markings, floppy/semi-floppy ears, and friendly
  face. It must not become a wolf, fox, husky, shepherd, long-legged guardian,
  or supernatural canine monster.

Evolution is newborn puppy → younger juvenile → adult Fatty:

- **Stage I — newborn Fatty:** Extremely small, round compact torso, very short
  legs, sleepy expression, soft puppy coat, minimal muzzle/beard development,
  and almost no ornamentation. This must read immediately as baby Fatty.
- **Stage II — young Fatty, approximately 3–4 months:** Clearly smaller and
  younger than Stage III, with shorter legs, rounder puppy proportions, younger
  face, less developed muzzle/beard, softer coat, playful juvenile expression,
  simpler celestial harness, and fewer Go-stone effects. It must not collapse
  into an adult-like intermediate.
- **Stage III — adult Fatty / mature final identity:** Preserve the Owner
  reference's recognizable scruffy black/charcoal dog, pale chest/paws, happy
  open-mouth expression, restrained precision harness, moon/Go symbolism, and
  black/white Go-stone orbit. The emotional result is “the Owner's real dog
  became a Go Odyssey Spirit,” not a generic legendary hound.

**Follower language:** compact playful trot and attentive head turn, with a
small Go-stone glint after a confirmed precision moment. It follows beside the
Hero and never signals an answer before settlement.

**Hero portrait:** Face-forward or three-quarter portrait prioritizing the
recognizable muzzle, scruffy coat, pale chest, and floppy ears. Harness/Go
stones remain secondary to real-dog identity across desktop, iPad, and mobile.

**Battle cue direction:** a restrained black/white Go-stone orbit pulse after
server settlement only; no best-move arrow, oracle symbol, answer highlight,
or automatic-correct implication.

### Spirit #6 — Obsidian Bastion

- **Role:** `SUPPORT`
- **Identity:** black/gold celestial guardian mech with Go defensive language.
- **Silhouette:** controlled humanoid mech, obsidian/black armor, ivory/gold
  trim, blue energy cores, shoulder structure, and a large Go-grid defensive
  shield carrying black/white stone motifs.

Evolution is awakened guardian core → growing guardian mech → final Obsidian
Bastion:

- **Stage I — newly awakened guardian core:** Very small rounded robot, compact
  limbs, friendly blue eyes, simplified armor, small defensive shield, and
  subtle Go-stone motif. It feels curious and newly activated.
- **Stage II — growing guardian mech:** Larger than Stage I with articulated
  limbs, emerging humanoid structure, stronger armor, recognizable Go-grid
  shield, and clearer black/white stones. It remains simpler and smaller than
  Stage III.
- **Stage III — Obsidian Bastion final guardian:** Preserve the Owner
  reference's controlled humanoid scale, black/ivory/gold structure, blue cores,
  strong shoulders, large Go-grid shield, and protective stance. No wings,
  cannons, extra shields, weapons, city-scale proportions, Titanization, or
  additional transformation mode.

**Follower language:** compact servo step, small blue eye pulse, and a single
shield-grid shimmer when a support moment settles. The companion stays beside
the Hero and never blocks zone nodes.

**Hero portrait:** Front three-quarter mech bust with blue eyes, gold trim,
and a cropped Go-grid shield. Owned/Locked/Active changes are presentation
states only; no effect or ownership authority is added.

**Battle cue direction:** brief blue defensive-grid pulse after server
settlement; it communicates support mood only and does not promise a result.
